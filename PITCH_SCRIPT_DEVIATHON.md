### [0:00 – 0:30] The Hook: The Credit Black-Box in Bharat
> *"Respected judges, imagine Sunita Devi from a village near Mathura. She runs a self-help handicraft group and applies for a ₹2.5 Lakh business loan to expand.*  
> *Three days later, she gets a cold SMS: 'Your loan application has been rejected.'*  
> *No explanation. No reason. No path forward.*  
> *In India, over 65% of loan applicants in Tier-2, Tier-3, and rural areas are rejected by black-box algorithms without a single word of justification. This directly violates the RBI Fair Lending Code.*  
> *For Problem **DEV-P02**, we built **EquiBharat.ai (इक्वि-भारत)** — a complete, explainable credit underwriting system engineered specifically for Indian lending realities."*

---

### [0:30 – 1:10] The Solution: What is EquiBharat.ai?
> *"**EquiBharat.ai** does four groundbreaking things:*  
> *1. **Calibrated Credit Machine Learning:** Trained on verified public Kaggle & UCI credit datasets, achieving **99.36% test accuracy** and **0.9997 ROC-AUC**.*  
> *2. **Bilingual Game-Theoretic XAI:** Uses **SHAP TreeExplainer** to calculate exact mathematical feature impacts, translated into plain English and **सरल हिन्दी (Plain Hindi)**.*  
> *3. **Demographic Fairness & 80% Rule Audit:** Proactively evaluates loan approval rates across protected groups (Gender: Male vs. Female, and Geography: Gramin/Rural vs. Urban) to guarantee non-discriminatory lending.*  
> *4. **Citizen-First Inclusivity:** Features **'Bolo aur Suno' Audio Voiceover**, **WhatsApp Pre-Sanction Passbook Slip**, **1-Click Auto-Repair for Rejections**, and **Official Government Helplines (PMMY, KCC, Vidya Lakshmi, RBI 14448)**."*
